Fun Mods

Mix and match. They are baked into the world when you create it.
Thunderstorms roll in with rain and lightning strikes, and tornadoes tear across the land ripping up blocks and throwing you into the sky.
Pitch-black nights you can barely see through, monsters that never burn in daylight, whispers in the dark and a Stalker that only moves when you are not looking.
Every block you break, mob you kill and chest you open gives a random item. About one in thirty is a jackpot with a whole gear set.
