Iron Battle Campaign Guide: Plan Across 1,000 Operations

Build repeatable habits for the long campaign, including special target missions.

What this mission is about

A very long campaign rewards routines that work across changing maps. Players need a consistent opening scan, a way to name priorities and a short record of lessons. Every tenth battle introduces a different target-style problem, so flexibility matters alongside a reliable core plan.

SKILL 2

SKILL 3

Recognizing when mission rules change

SKILL 4

Maintaining a long-term challenge log

Set up the mission

Create a campaign notebook with one line per operation: opening objective, key unit choice, turning point and next adjustment. Keep entries short enough to complete after every session. Iron Battle is a real-time strategy game about units, objectives, supply and support choices.

Challenge steps

Begin each operation by locating headquarters, objectives and major crossings.

Choose an opening objective based on route and supply value.

Keep a small reserve until the enemy direction is clearer.

Record the turning point immediately after the operation.

For target missions, slow down and read the mission-specific objective before applying normal battle habits.

Every five operations, review the log for a repeated mistake.

Talk it through

A short explanation turns an exciting moment into a chance to organize evidence and ideas. Ask one or two questions instead of turning the whole session into a quiz.

  • Which opening works across the most maps?
  • What clue signals that the standard plan should change?
  • How does the special mission alter unit value?

Level it up

Write a five-operation briefing that predicts likely needs without pretending to know each map. Afterward, highlight accurate predictions and surprises.

A note for parents and homeschool adults

Set the session length and learning goal before play, then leave room for the child’s own solution. Treat the game as a prompt for practice and questions. Verify history and science claims with reliable outside sources. Ironcraft public multiplayer can include other players and chat, so supervise online play and use a private room for a known group.
