Quick answers

Is Iron Games free?

Yes. All three games are completely free to play, with no account, no download and nothing to buy. Coins and unlocks inside the games are earned by playing.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Each game is a single web page. Open it in your browser and it loads in seconds.

What games can I play on Iron Games?

Iron Front is top-down WW2 tank combat. Iron Battle is real-time strategy on a tactical map. Ironcraft is a browser-based voxel survival sandbox. Iron Hook is a relaxing fishing game. Iron Jaws is a shark hunt through twenty sea caves in the Hunt the Wumpus tradition. Iron Trail is an Oregon Trail style wagon journey to Oregon. Iron Rocks is an Asteroids style space shooter. Iron Serpent is snake with golden apples and iron walls. Iron Hammer has you mine ore, forge swords and fight medieval battles. Iron Paws is a cozy pet shelter, Iron Bakery a cake shop and Iron Style a fashion studio with runway challenges. Iron Garden is a cozy garden, Iron Aquarium a fish tank, Iron Bouquet a flower shop, Iron Room a bedroom designer and Iron Meadow a butterfly-catching walk. Iron Jigsaw, Iron Memory and Iron Gems are jigsaw, matching-pairs and match-three puzzles, Iron Colors is colour-by-number pixel art and Iron Tunes is a music maker. Iron Guns is a first-person 3D battle royale on a huge island with hills, towns, bridges and an airfield: loot, build, drive cars, fly fighter jets and bombers and outlast the closing storm. Iron Door is a first-person backrooms horror game: an endless maze of yellow rooms, entities that hunt you, almond water for sanity and a hidden door down on each of five levels.

Can I play Ironcraft with friends?

Yes. Click Multiplayer on the Ironcraft title screen to see a live list of public worlds you can join without a code, create your own public world, or open a private room and share its code. Players connect directly to each other through the browser.

Which browsers and devices work?

Current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on computers, tablets and phones. Computer players use a keyboard and mouse; mobile players use the on-screen controls. Ironcraft requires WebGL2 and works best in landscape orientation.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Worlds, unlocks and coins are saved in your browser on the device you play on, so come back to the same browser to continue.

What are the Ironcraft fun mods?

Optional modes you pick when creating a world: Weather with thunderstorms and EF1 to EF5 tornadoes, Horror, Random Blocks, Hardcore, Structure Chaos, Zombie Apocalypse waves, Guns (guns and ammo crafted only on a Gun Table, scopes, suppressors, foregrips, extended mags and laser sights fitted on an Attachment Table), Hearts (Minecraft-style pixel hearts), Night Vision, Real Life (big falls knock you out), Uncrafting (a table that takes crafted items back apart), Leaf Doors (doors and grass pressure plates crafted from leaves), No Name Tags (hidden player names in multiplayer), Nukes (craftable nukes with timers from ten seconds to thirty minutes, a Missile Silo with a targeting map you can zoom, and nuke bombs for the B-27), Camping (campfires that cook food and keep monsters away, Toasted Marshmallows and S'mores, tents, sleeping bags, lanterns and a nine-slot backpack) and War (a war kit at the start, vehicles at real-life size, patrol boats and destroyers on the water, and more guns, including a flak cannon). They can be mixed.

Does Ironcraft have vehicles?

Yes. Craft a Car, a Fighter Jet or a B-27 Bomber from iron and glass on a crafting table, place it and right-click to get in. Cars drive with W and steer with the mouse, jets take off once they are rolling fast enough and fire a twin cannon, the B-27 bomber seats a pilot, two turret gunners and a bombardier and drops the TNT or nukes you load into its bay, and a tank with a turret and explosive shells comes from its own spawn egg or /spawn tank. Shift gets you out. Several players can share a vehicle's seats in multiplayer, including on public servers: right-click boards the first free seat, V moves to the next free seat, and seat 0 drives or flies. In War worlds vehicles are real-life size (a car is 1.8 times normal size, a tank 2.5 times, a jet or bomber 3 times), and the naval ships, a Patrol Boat and a Destroyer, float on the water and stay on it. There are also a Helicopter, a Submarine with torpedoes, an A-10 Warthog and a Mortar, every vehicle needs a crafted Engine, and you see your vehicle from behind while driving (P switches to the cockpit).

How do I find diamonds or a village in Ironcraft?

Press T to open chat and type a teleport command such as /tp diamonds, /tp village, /tp trial chamber, /tp ancient city or /tp sift (the Sift dimension). It finds the closest one and takes you there.

Who makes Iron Games?

Daniele Bros. The games are updated regularly with new features, and the site lives at irongames.win.

How to play Ironcraft with friends
  1. Open Ironcraft and click Multiplayer on the title screen.
  2. For a private room, enter a nickname that is not your real name.
  3. Pick a public world from the live list to join it instantly, or click Create New Public World to host one for everyone.
  4. For a private game, host a room and share its code only with people you know.
  5. Press T in the game to chat. Blocks, dropped items, chests, weather and zombie waves are shared.
