IRON BLITZ

3D TANK BATTLES · 7 VS 7

Drive with WASD or the arrow keys, aim with the mouse (click to lock it), left click or Space to fire, right click or Shift for the sniper scope, mouse wheel to zoom. On phones: left stick drives, drag the right side to aim, FIRE and ZOOM buttons. The reticle turns green where you can penetrate, yellow where it is close and red where you will bounce. Win by destroying every enemy or holding the enemy base circle until it is captured.

Tank stats are game approximations in the spirit of WoT Blitz, not historical figures. Matchmaking puts you with tanks one tier above or below yours.
