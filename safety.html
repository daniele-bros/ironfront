A QUICK GUIDE FOR FAMILIES

PLAY SAFE. PLAY KIND.

Iron Front and Iron Battle are single-player games. Ironcraft also has multiplayer. These simple rules help everyone choose the right way to play.

The safest multiplayer choice

Use Host Private World and share the short room code only with family and friends you already know. A private code limits who can join, but players should still avoid sharing personal information in chat.

How public rooms work

Anyone online can join a public Ironcraft room. Public rooms therefore use a random player alias, hide names supplied by other players and disable chat. Building and gameplay remain shared. Children should ask a parent or guardian before entering a public room.

Never share these details

IDENTITY

Real name, age, photographs or passwords

CONTACT

Email, phone number or social-media account

LOCATION

Home address, school, team or regular schedule

Community rules

  • Be kind and give other players room to build and explore.
  • Do not damage another player’s work unless everyone agreed to that kind of game.
  • Do not impersonate another person or ask for private information.
  • Leave immediately and tell a trusted adult if anything feels uncomfortable.
  • Use a private room when playing with siblings, classmates or a homeschool group.

Healthy play

Agree on a stopping time before playing, take regular movement and eye breaks, and use the Activity Hub when a family wants a goal that combines play with writing, math, history or design.

Choose a game

Play solo or use a private Ironcraft room with people you know.
