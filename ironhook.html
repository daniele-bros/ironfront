IRON HOOK

Cast · Hook · Reel

A free fishing game in your browser. Start on a quiet mill pond with a bamboo rod and a tin of worms, earn coins for every catch, and work your way out to the sea and the abyss. Twenty-four species to log, day and night, rain, records to beat.

CAST · hold the mouse button (or Space) to charge, release to throw. Longer casts reach deeper, wilder water.
HOOK · the hook sinks on its own. Hold W or the up arrow to lift it, S to let it drop. Different fish live at different depths.
BITE · when the rod bends, click or press Space fast to set the hook.
REEL · keep the green catch bar over the fish: hold to raise it, let go to drop. Fill the progress bar before the fish wears you out.
ESC · reel in an empty hook. B shop, L fish log, Tab switch bait.
